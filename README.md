# Women's Clothing Sales Data Analysis

A data analysis project exploring sales performance, revenue trends, store performance, product performance, payment methods, and sales patterns using Excel.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes women's clothing sales data to identify business trends, measure sales performance, and generate actionable insights.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was prepared through several data cleaning and transformation steps:

- Handled missing values.
- Standardized and formatted the data.
- Standardized the Size column using consistent capitalization.
- Added a calculated Total Sales column using Quantity × Price.
- Categorized invoices into High, Medium, and Low value groups.
- Categorized quantities into Big, Normal, and Small groups.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project explores:

- Number of invoices by store.
- Invoice distribution by category.
- Product sales across different stores and cities.
- Best-selling products by store.
- Most frequently used payment methods.
- Total revenue by store.
- Total revenue by product.
- Revenue trends by date.
- Sales and profit trends by year and month.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights:

- The dataset contains 206 total orders with total revenue of 312,985.4.
- Omdurman generated the highest revenue at 128,811.5, followed by Bahri at 114,188 and Khartoum at 69,985.9.
- Shoes were the top-performing product category, generating 111,511.5 in revenue, approximately 35.6% of total revenue.
- Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 111 orders (53.9%).
- June 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue at 57,236.5.
- Revenue was higher in 2024 (182,069.9) than in 2025 (130,915.5) within the analyzed period.
